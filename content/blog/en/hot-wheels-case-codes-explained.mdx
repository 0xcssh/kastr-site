When collectors say "the K case just hit Walmart", they are talking about Mattel's shipping waves. Knowing the case letter tells you which cars to expect on the pegs, and which Treasure Hunt and Super Treasure Hunt could be hiding among them.

## What a case is

Mainline Hot Wheels, the basic cars sold one at a time, leave the factory in sealed cases of 72 cars. Each year Mattel splits its mainline range into about fifteen waves and gives each one a letter. Every case of a given letter carries the same mix of cars, so two stores that receive "2026 K" stock the same models.

The letters run from A to Q and skip I and O, so a full year has fifteen cases: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, P and Q.

## Why the letter matters to a hunter

Each case letter has its own Treasure Hunt and its own Super Treasure Hunt. If you know that the L case is on the shelf, you know exactly which two hunts could be in it, and you stop looking for cars that belong to other cases.

The [2026 case tracker](/en/cases/2026) lists each case with its Treasure Hunt and Super, their toy numbers and colors. The [2027 page](/en/cases/2027) fills in as Mattel reveals the next cases: the reveals run ahead of the stores, so the first 2027 cases were already known in November 2026.

## How to tell which case is in your store

- **The cars themselves.** Each case brings a batch of new models. If you see two or three cars from the same new batch, the case is probably the one they belong to. The yearly lists show which case each hunt came with.
- **The back of the card.** Collector guides say the code next to the toy number on US cards ends with the case letter. International cards do not print it.
- **The factory box.** A sealed 72-car case shows its letter on the label, which is how collectors who buy whole cases know what they hold.

## How many hunts per case

In recent years every case letter has had one Treasure Hunt and one Super Treasure Hunt. A store case usually holds one Treasure Hunt, while the Super is far rarer: sources disagree on how many cases contain one, and collectors report that US cases get more of them than international ones. Nobody outside Mattel knows the exact ratio, so be wary of anyone who quotes a precise figure.

## Store dates

Mattel does not publish when each case reaches stores, and dates differ by country, by chain and even by store. That is why the case tracker shows the order of the cases and the next letter to come, but never a date it would have to guess.
