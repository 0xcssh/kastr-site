We make Kastr, so this comparison is not neutral. We have tried to be fair: each app below gets what it does better than us, and the facts come from their App Store pages and public reviews. Ratings are from the US App Store on 10 November 2026; apps change fast, so check before you choose.

## Die Carz

The app to beat. It covers Hot Wheels from 1968 to today plus many other brands, works on iPhone, Android and the web, and has an active community. Its scanner is polished: photos are queued while you keep shooting, and each result comes with candidates and a match percentage. Since October 2026 it shows values with charts and the sales behind them.

**Where it falls short:** AI scans are limited to 30 credits a month on the free plan, and when we looked, prices showed in dollars even outside the US. **What it does better than Kastr:** Android and web, years of reviews, a much larger community.

## Hunt64

A well-rated app with a loyal following, especially in Turkey. It flags doubles, filters Treasure Hunts and Supers, and lets collectors trade their doubles. Its developer answers reviews quickly and says the AI scans are free.

**Where it falls short:** the free collection is capped, the total value of a collection is in the Pro plan, and the community price only appears once five collectors have entered one. **What it does better than Kastr:** trading doubles between collectors is further along.

## iCollect Toy Cars

The veteran, with the most ratings of the group. It scans bar codes and keeps lists with photos and values.

**Where it falls short:** reviews mention a free limit around fifteen cars, values that are off, and scans that mismatch. On mainline cards the bar code is shared by a whole case, which limits what a bar code scan can tell. **What it does better than Kastr:** a long track record.

## HW Price Guide

As the name says, it is about prices, sold by subscription. One reviewer calls it the best for values.

**Where it falls short:** reviews mention full-screen ads and prices hidden behind the paywall.

## Kastr

Our app, launching on the iPhone in December 2026. It is built for the aisle: it reads the toy number on the card on the phone itself, finds the exact version among 87,922 cars from ten brands, and shows at once whether you already own it and what it is worth. The catalog works offline. Search and the value of each car are free, with no ads; Pro adds unlimited scans, the total value of the garage and its history, several garages and release alerts. It imports a collection from a spreadsheet or another app's export.

**Where it falls short:** it is new, with no reviews yet, no Android version until after launch, and a community that is just starting.

## Which one to choose

- **You want the most complete app today, on any device:** Die Carz.
- **You want to trade your doubles:** Hunt64.
- **You hunt in stores and want to know in a second if you own a car and what it is worth:** Kastr, once it is out.
