Which 2026 Hot Wheels are worth the most? For the cars you can find in stores, the answer is the year's Super Treasure Hunts, followed by its Treasure Hunts. Their order changes every week with listings, so we do not print it in stone: the ranking below is recalculated from live eBay listings every time you open this page.

## What puts a car at the top

- **Being a Super.** Each case letter, A to Q, has one Super Treasure Hunt, with Spectraflame paint and Real Riders rubber tires. They are made in far smaller numbers than the mainline cars and lead any ranking of the year.
- **The real car behind it.** Classic American muscle, Japanese tuners and famous movie cars draw more buyers than fantasy designs made in the same numbers.
- **When it was found.** A Super from an early case has had months on the market; one from the latest case can sit high while few copies are out.
- **Condition.** These values are for carded cars. A soft corner or a cracked blister brings the price down.

## Treasure Hunts

The regular Treasure Hunts, with their silver flame, sit well below the Supers: most sell for a few dollars above a mainline car. A popular model can still climb higher. The full lists, with photos and case letters, are on the [2026 Super Treasure Hunt list](/en/super-treasure-hunt/2026) and the [2026 Treasure Hunt list](/en/treasure-hunt/2026).

## Read the ranking with care

A ranking of asking prices is a snapshot, not a forecast. One seller can move a car up for a day, and prices for the latest cases often settle once more copies reach collectors. Use it to know what a find is worth today, not to predict what it will be worth tomorrow.
